What changed from the 4-slide version —

Dropped: Slide 3 "Say it your way" (feature grid — Photos, Voice, GIFs, React). Users discover these in the input bar naturally; showing them pre-chat adds length without adding trust.

Arc now: Who you are (anonymous, real people) → What it costs to try (nothing, no account) → Who's in charge (you, with free credits to start). Each slide answers one question a new user actually has before committing.

Unchanged: All visual design, palette, CTA button, dot style, skip behavior. Drop-in replacement — only content changes.
