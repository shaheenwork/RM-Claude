Top 3 picks · with trade-offs

Three I'd take into refinement first, each optimizing for a different brand direction.

PICK 1 · Safest premium bet
01 · "മ" Mark
Win: highest scalability, ownable, instantly Malayali, works across every surface (icon, splash, merch). Trade: needs the saffron dot + a strong wordmark to feel "random" — pure letter alone reads serious.
PICK 2 · Most "Gen-Z share"
02 · Coco-Die
Win: the cleanest pun in the set — "random + Malayali" lands in one second, screenshots well, meme-able. Trade: light-on-cream isn't as bold as dark backgrounds in a crowded home screen; may need a dark variant for app-icon visibility.
PICK 3 · Best NRI / diaspora
09 · Kasavu Wink
Win: the most "looks at home next to global apps" while staying unmistakably Malayali via kasavu. The wink carries the cheeky tone. Trade: cream background can disappear on white app-store cards — needs a tested dark variant.
