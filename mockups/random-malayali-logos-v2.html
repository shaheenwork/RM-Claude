Top 3 picks · with trade-offs

All three are brand-aligned. Each wins on a different axis.

PICK 1 · Most ownable
10 · Negative മ
Win: single-mark identity. Carries chat + Malayali + brand gradient in one ownable cutout. Nothing on any app store looks like this. Trade: requires കthe letter to render cleanly at small sizes — Malayalam typography must be tuned, not just dropped in.
PICK 2 · Clearest read
02 · Bubble മ
Win: instantly says "chat app" + "Malayali" — zero ambiguity, works in any context. Safest path to product-market fit on the icon. Trade: closest to "generic chat icon" of the set — less distinctive than #10 unless the bubble shape gets a custom tweak.
PICK 3 · Most Gen-Z
06 · Hello Blob
Win: character-led mark with the highest "share and meme" potential. Builds a mascot you can extend across stickers, loading screens, OG images. Trade: character-led identities are harder to take seriously in older diaspora — may need a more restrained variant for premium contexts.
